  1. 1. Select a trade route — choose origin, destination, product type, and sensitivity.
  2. 2. Review risk signals — examine fuel, political, labour, sanctions, and weather factors.
  3. 3. Compare alternatives — review route scores and contributing factors side by side.
  4. 4. Export and share — prepare a PDF, CSV, or print-ready summary.
